01关于我 About Me

这里是 BG8LXU，常驻坐标网格 OL39do。一名把业余无线电、胶片摄影和一堆奇怪的爱好塞进同一具身体里的工程师。

喜欢在 24 楼阳台上折腾天线，也喜欢在频道里偶遇陌生人；除了通联，我还热衷于交换明信片与收集邮票——所以如果你也喜欢纸质的、需要等待的东西，我们大概聊得来。

居住于重庆，有时位于青岛。重庆是山城，我的 5.6 米 GP 就竖在山与江的夹缝里，靠天吃饭，也靠电离层赏脸。欢迎任何波段、任何模式、任何语言的 QSO，来卡必回。

平时短波以 FT8 为主，常出现在 14 / 18 / 21 MHz（20 m / 17 m / 15 m）；U/V 段则用模拟语音通联，并接收卫星与 SSTV。非常希望能与更多国际爱好者通联交流——中文或英文都可以，简单的英文也完全没问题。

BG8LXU here, permanently in grid OL39do — Chongqing, China, and sometimes Qingdao. An engineer who squeezed amateur radio, film photography and a pile of odd hobbies into one body. I like messing with antennas on a 24th-floor balcony and running into strangers on the air. Apart from radio, I collect and exchange postcards and stamps — if you like paper things that take time to arrive, we'll get along.

On HF I mostly work FT8, typically on 14 / 18 / 21 MHz (20 m / 17 m / 15 m). On V/UHF I use analog voice and receive satellites and SSTV. I would love to work and talk with more international hams — Chinese or English, and simple English is perfectly fine. Any band, any mode, any language is welcome. All QSL cards will be answered.
